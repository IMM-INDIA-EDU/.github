# Institute of Marketing and Management (IMM)

### Human Centred. Future Focused.

**Shaping future managers, entrepreneurs and business leaders since 1969.**

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the **Institute of Marketing and Management (IMM), New Delhi, India.**

Established in 1969, IMM is a management education institution with a legacy of developing business professionals and future leaders through value-based education, industry engagement, academic excellence and holistic development.

With a global alumni network of over 20,000 professionals, IMM continues its commitment to preparing students for an evolving business world.

🌐 **Website:** [immindia.edu.in](https://immindia.edu.in/)

## About IMM

IMM believes in combining strong management fundamentals with contemporary business practices, professional values and practical learning.

Its educational approach focuses on developing individuals who can think critically, make informed decisions, understand changing business environments and contribute meaningfully to organisations and society.

### Our Vision

To serve as a Centre for Excellence in Management Education, Research and Training by carrying forward IMM's legacy with young leaders and nation builders, blending modern management thought with enduring values.

### Our Mission

To impart value-based management education through an interdisciplinary approach, integrating teaching with research, training, seminars, conferences and publications, while contributing to India's presence on the global marketing and management map.

## Academic Programmes

IMM offers management programmes designed to develop business knowledge, professional competence and leadership capabilities.

### Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM)

The two-year, full-time PGDM is IMM's flagship management programme, approved by AICTE.

The programme offers dual specialisations, allowing students to develop expertise across complementary areas of management.

**Available specialisations:**

- Marketing & Innovation Management
- Financial Management
- Human Resource Management
- Business Analytics & Research
- International Business Management
- Operations & Supply Chain Management
- Entrepreneurship Management

The curriculum combines management fundamentals, business analytics, emerging business practices, industry exposure and professional development.

### Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

IMM's BBA programme focuses on building a strong foundation in business administration and management.

The programme incorporates contemporary business learning, practical projects, professional development, industry exposure and emerging areas of business and technology.

## Learning Beyond the Classroom

At IMM, education extends beyond academic coursework. Students are encouraged to develop practical knowledge, professional confidence and a broader understanding of the corporate world.

Key learning experiences include:

- **Industry Interaction:** Guest lectures, corporate interactions and sessions with industry professionals.
- **Live Projects:** Opportunities to apply classroom concepts to real-world business challenges.
- **Internships:** Practical industry exposure and opportunities to develop professional experience.
- **Corporate Skill Development:** Communication, presentation, personality development and workplace readiness.
- **International Exposure:** Academic collaborations and opportunities to understand global business perspectives.
- **Experiential Learning:** Activities, projects and learning initiatives that encourage practical application.

## Student Life & Campus Experience

IMM encourages a vibrant student environment through clubs, activities, events and opportunities for collaboration.

The campus experience includes:

- Student clubs and extracurricular activities
- Cultural and academic events
- Corporate Connect initiatives
- Alumni interactions
- Industry visits and guest sessions
- Personality development activities

These experiences contribute to students' overall growth, confidence and professional readiness.

## Corporate Connect & Placements

IMM's Corporate Connect Hub works towards strengthening relationships between students, alumni, recruiters and industry professionals.

The institute provides placement and internship support, helping students explore career opportunities across different business sectors.

**Key areas of corporate engagement include:**

- Corporate networking and industry interactions
- Internship opportunities
- Final placement assistance
- Career guidance and professional preparation
- Alumni and recruiter engagement

Students pursue opportunities across sectors including BFSI, consulting, FMCG, logistics, manufacturing, retail and fintech.
